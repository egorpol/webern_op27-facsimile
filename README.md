# Webern, Variations for Piano, Op. 27 — page images of the Universal Edition print

The nine music pages of Anton Webern's *Variationen für Klavier*, Op. 27, Universal Edition, Vienna, plate U.E. 10881 (first edition 1937), as 300-dpi PNG renderings of the scan in [IMSLP file 28271](https://imslp.org/wiki/Special:ImagefromIndex/28271). The copy scanned was printed from the original plates in 1965 or later.

The images serve the facsimile view of the MEI edition [egorpol/webern_op27](https://github.com/egorpol/webern_op27) ([viewer](https://egorpol.github.io/webern_op27/)). They carry no annotations; the bar regions are in the edition's MEI files.

## Copyright status

Anton Webern died in 1945. The print is in the public domain in the EU and in other countries where copyright lasts 70 years after the author's death or less. **In the United States it is still protected:** it was published in 1937 and its copyright was renewed in 1965, so it remains under copyright there until the end of 2032. IMSLP lists the file as "Public Domain - Non-PD US". Use these images only where the print is in the public domain.

The scan was uploaded to IMSLP by JacopoTore (2017); the scanner is unknown. The owner of this repository claims no rights in the images. Rights holders with concerns can open an issue.
